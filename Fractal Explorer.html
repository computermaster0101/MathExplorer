Smoothing

Smoothing kicks in when you stop moving. Very deep zooms with thousands of iterations use less, so the picture doesn't freeze.

Precision (Mandelbrot and Julia dives)

Standard goes to about ×10²⁸ and runs well on phones. Extreme carries about 125 digits and rescales every pixel's numbers so they never get too small for the graphics card, reaching about ×10¹⁰⁰. Each step deeper needs more steps per pixel (up to 100,000), so past about ×10⁴⁰ expect a second or more per frame even on a desktop. When you stop moving, the sharp picture is drawn a strip at a time.
